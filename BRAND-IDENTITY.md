# Absyon Digital — Brand Identity

## 1. Who we are

Absyon Digital is an AI-forward digital studio: a small team that designs, builds, and ships websites, apps, and AI-powered tools. We lead with technology — automation, AI chat agents, AI voice agents — and prove it with real, shipped work for real businesses.

**Tagline:** We build what's next.

## 2. Positioning

Lead with AI/tech. The portfolio (mostly independent UK food & hospitality businesses, plus ecommerce and services) is the proof of range and execution, not the headline. The pitch: Absyon Digital is where cutting-edge AI/automation meets the discipline of actually shipping working products for small, real businesses — not just AI theater.

**Services:**
- Web design & development
- App / software development
- Branding & graphic design
- AI automation solutions
- AI chat agents
- AI voice agents
- Menu design

**Audience:** Broad in principle, but the proof points skew toward independent local businesses (food & hospitality especially) and small-to-mid businesses that want a modern, tech-forward partner rather than a template agency.

**Team:** Small team (not solo) — the site should read as "we," not "I."

## 3. Voice & tone

- **Bold / edgy**, **futuristic / cutting-edge** as personality.
- **Confident & direct** as writing style — short, punchy statements, no filler, no agency-speak clichés ("synergy," "passionate team of creatives").
- Say the thing once, say it well. Prefer concrete claims (what was built, what it does) over vague adjectives.

**Inspiration references:**
- [bamlab.ch](https://bamlab.ch/en/) — minimal, editorial, one-voice confidence, numbered project index, work-first layout.
- [digitz.fr](https://digitz.fr/) — dark theme, numbered section system (01–09), methodology-driven, "AI as a layer, not a new job" positioning.

Absyon should borrow the *structural confidence* of both (numbered sections, work-first, no fluff) while keeping its own bold neon-on-black visual identity rather than copying either site's look.

## 4. Visual identity

### Logo
Dark/black background, glowing cyan constellation/network icon (four connected nodes in a circle around a star), bold wordmark "ABSYON" in white-to-cyan gradient with a lighter "DIGITAL" subtext beneath. Network/connectivity motif — reinforce this visually across the site (connecting lines, nodes, subtle particle/network animations).

### Color palette

| Role | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary background | Black | `#000000` (or near-black `#050505` for large surfaces) |
| Primary accent (bright blue glow) | Azure blue | `#1EA3FD` |
| Secondary accent (deeper blue, for text/borders on light areas) | Deep blue | `#0A8AD8` |
| Text on dark | Off-white | `#F2FFFF` |
| Muted text / secondary copy | Cool gray | `#9CA8A8` |

Keep the palette minimal — black, blue, white — per brand decision. No purple/indigo. Use blue glow/neon effects sparingly for emphasis (buttons, key stats, hover states, icon accents), not as a flat fill everywhere.

### Typography

- **Headings:** Space Grotesk (bold weights) — geometric, modern, echoes the logo's angular wordmark.
- **Body:** Inter — clean, highly readable at small sizes, pairs neutrally with Space Grotesk.

### Motion & feel

Futuristic/bold personality suggests: subtle glow/hover effects on interactive elements, network-node micro-animations (echoing the logo), smooth scroll-triggered reveals. Avoid anything twee or soft — no rounded pastel illustrations, no bouncy playful easing. Motion should feel precise and confident.
